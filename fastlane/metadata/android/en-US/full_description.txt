Sprout is a habit tracker built on what the evidence actually shows about forming habits — and on
what it shows about the things that make trackers feel bad to use.

Tracking is the active ingredient, so logging is one tap from a widget, a notification, or the app.

Nothing ever resets to zero. Your headline number is a strength score that grows as you show up
and dents — but never wipes — when you miss. Streaks come with slack: rest days that you earn by
showing up, spent silently when you need them, and a 48-hour window to earn a run back.

The day after a miss is the most rewarded day in the app. There are no guilt notifications, no
"you lost your streak" messages, and misses are never coloured red.

Every gamified mechanic has an off switch.

No account. No analytics. No network access by default. Your data is a file you own.
